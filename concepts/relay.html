THE BUSINESS BEHIND THE DESIGN / ELVARNUE

Give every enquiry a clear next step.

Sales and operations teams moving enquiries between forms, spreadsheets and a CRM.

The problem this addresses

Manual copying can leave leads unassigned or create duplicate records. A defined workflow checks incoming information and routes it to the appropriate next step.

Explore the interactions

  • Run a sample enquiry through validation, duplicate checks and budget-based routing.
  • Change routing rules and compare valid, invalid and lower-budget inputs.
  • Simulate a failed CRM step, retry it and inspect execution history.

What we could deliver for you

A production scope can cover agreed API connections, field mapping, routing rules, exception handling, monitoring and an operating guide.

What we need to get started

The tools you use, sample field structures, routing rules, expected volumes and who should handle exceptions.
