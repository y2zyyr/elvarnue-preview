Fieldnotes is an imagined independent publication about slower journeys and a more attentive everyday. It is built around original sample essays, evocative concept imagery and a reading experience that makes space for the story.

These are fictional scenes and reflections, not destination guides, reported travel stories or advice about a specific place. All photography is illustrative concept imagery.
