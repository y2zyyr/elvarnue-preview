INCLUDED / DEFECT CORRECTION

30 days of defect correction.

For 30 calendar days from written final acceptance, we correct reproducible defects in our delivered work that prevent agreed features from operating as specified, without an additional development fee. New preferences or requirements are assessed as changes, not defects.

SUPPORT / RESPONSE

Acknowledge. Assess. Agree.

We aim to acknowledge support requests within two business days, Monday–Friday, China Standard Time (UTC+8), excluding public holidays. Severe incidents, such as a delivered system becoming entirely unavailable, receive priority during working hours. Cause, severity and the resolution schedule are assessed separately; acknowledgement is not a fix-time guarantee.

OPTIONAL / SEPARATE SCOPE

Ongoing care as you need it.

Maintenance can cover agreed updates, monitoring, backups or small changes. New features, content edits, third-party outages, API changes and issues caused by unauthorised modifications are assessed separately. If an issue is caused by a defect in our implementation, it remains covered by the applicable defect correction terms. No 24/7 service is included by default.

Help us reproduce the issue.

Share the affected URL or workflow, steps to reproduce, expected and actual behaviour, browser or device, and screenshots where useful. Do not include passwords or confidential customer records.

What does this portfolio show?＋

These are independent design concepts showing our approach to websites, software and automation.

Can you work with my industry?＋

We can discuss businesses beyond the industries shown here. Share your services, audience and a few reference websites so we can assess the fit and define an appropriate scope.

What does a project cost?＋

Projects are quoted in USD after we review the page count, features, content and integrations. Your proposal separates the agreed delivery from third-party subscriptions, hosting and optional ongoing support.

How long will it take?＋

As an indicative guide: small business websites take 2–4 weeks, focused SaaS MVPs 6–12 weeks, and bounded automations 1–3 weeks after scope approval, the agreed initial payment and required inputs are in place. These are estimates, not delivery guarantees; your proposal defines the schedule.

What do I need to provide?＋

A business overview, your goals, brand assets, available content and feedback at agreed review points. For SaaS, add the user roles and most important tasks; for automation, list the tools and steps involved. If you need help with structure, copy or visual assets, we can include that in the scope. Access is arranged securely after scope approval; do not send passwords through this form.

What happens after launch?＋

Our delivery standard includes agreed handover materials and a 30-calendar-day defect correction window from written final acceptance. Ongoing maintenance and new work are scoped separately. Ownership, licences, exclusions and response arrangements are finalised in the contract.

How do we work across time zones?＋

Our team is based in China, UTC+8. Written briefs, shared previews and weekly written updates keep decisions visible between review points. Feedback deadlines and any live meeting windows are agreed for the project, so collaboration does not depend on everyone being online at the same time.

Can you improve an existing website or system?＋

We can assess an existing website, product or workflow. Share what works, what needs to change and the tools involved. We confirm what can be retained, rebuilt or connected before estimating the work.

Can we work in my language?＋

You can explore the site and prepare your brief in any of the six website languages. We agree the language for project documents, meetings and feedback before work begins.

Can our team manage the result after handover?＋

We agree who will edit content or operate the system, then scope the appropriate admin tools and guidance. The delivery checklist identifies source files, access and third-party licences. Optional maintenance is quoted separately.

Saving keeps a brief on this device. Text drafts last for this browser session. Downloading creates a file you can keep or share.
